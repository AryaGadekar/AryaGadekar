## About

My work blends careful analysis with readable visuals. I focus on clarity, impact, and making the complex feel ordinary. Outside of work I travel, read, and collect short conversations that linger.

## Featured Projects

**Blinkit Retail Performance & Revenue Analytics Dashboard**  
A full retail analytics solution that tracks product-level revenue, fill rate, pricing elasticity, and promotional ROI. I integrated disparate sales and inventory feeds, built scheduled ETL to reduce manual reporting, and created executive dashboards that cut reporting time and clarified levers to improve margin.

**Hotel Portfolio Revenue & Performance Intelligence**  
A revenue intelligence platform across multiple properties. I modeled demand by channel and segment, produced forecasts tied to rate strategies, and built visual tools for daily decision making. The output helped prioritize initiatives that improved occupancy and top line performance.

What this really means is simple. I deliver analysis that finds cost saving opportunities, surface-level visualizations that turn into strategy, and presentations that lead to action.
